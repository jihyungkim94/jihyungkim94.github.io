Biography

I am a lecturer in the Department of AI Convergence at Sahmyook University in Seoul, where I teach applied deep learning, data science, data structures and algorithms, and AI literacy. I hold an M.C.S. from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

Before returning to academia I worked in corporate strategic planning at Stealien, a cybersecurity company. Earlier, I was a research assistant in the Socio-Technical Interaction Research Lab at the University of Central Florida, where I worked on co-designing online safety interventions with teens, and in the Future Reality Lab at the University of Arizona.
